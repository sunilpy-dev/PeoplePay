## Brand & Style

This design system embodies corporate authority, operational clarity, and unwavering precision tailored for enterprise human capital management and payroll execution. Operating at the intersection of regulatory rigor and human workforce engagement, the interface projects stability, total auditability, and immediate comprehension under high-density workflows.

### Design Movement
**Corporate Modernism meets Swiss Functionalism.** The aesthetic rejects superficial decorations, heavy skeuomorphic effects, and decorative noise. It relies on architectural structural grids, deliberate typographic scale, hairline structural borders, and subtle tonal separation. Every visual element exists to accelerate comprehension, minimize transactional error, and sustain high cognitive focus over long operational sessions.

### Emotional Response & Target Audience
Built for enterprise HR directors, global payroll specialists, compensation analysts, and compliance officers, the system induces calm control and absolute confidence. When processing multi-million-dollar payroll runs, cross-border tax compliance, or intricate compensation modeling, users feel anchored by quiet, frictionless predictability.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate sense of institutional reliability. Deep slate navy anchors core navigation, key headers, and dominant interface landmarks, while crisp neutrals provide clean, optical whitespace for high-density tabular calculation.

### Palette Roles & Distribution
- **Dominant Primary (`#0F172A` - Slate Navy):** The structural backbone. Applied to root shell navigation, primary action buttons, persistent top-bars, and critical metric readouts. It provides contrast and authority without the harshness of pure black.
- **Secondary Action (`#2563EB` - Cobalt Indigo):** Used for focal interactivity, active state indicators, linked records, and active selection tabs.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#0D9488` - Deep Teal):** Communicates financial operations, approved calculations, timecard authorizations, and verified compensation structures.
- **Surface Foundations:**
  - Background Canvas: `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50)
  - Surface Card/Panel: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
  - Surface Subdued/Track: `#F1F5F9` (Slate 100)
  - Border Structural: `#E2E8F0` (Slate 200)
  - Border Focus/Divider: `#CBD5E1` (Slate 300)
- **Text & Semantics:**
  - Primary Text: `#0F172A` (Slate 900)
  - Secondary / Metadata: `#334155` (Slate 700)
  - Muted / Placeholder: `#64748B` (Slate 500)
- **Status & Risk Indicators:**
  - Success / Settled: `#10B981` (Emerald) | Surface tint: `#ECFDF5`
  - Warning / Verification Required: `#F59E0B` (Amber) | Surface tint: `#FFFBEB`
  - Critical / Tax Discrepancy / Breach: `#E11D48` (Rose) | Surface tint: `#FFF1F2`

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy prioritizes legibility, scannability, and structural rigidity. 

- **Primary Interface Typeface (Hanken Grotesk):** Chosen for its balance of geometric crispness and humanist clarity. Its large x-height and open counters maintain razor-sharp definition even across micro-labels and compressed table headers.
- **Data & Monospace Typeface (JetBrains Mono):** Reserved for financial amounts, tax code IDs, timestamps, banking account hashes, and currency calculations. It guarantees that financial sums align down the decimal point across multi-row payroll grids without horizontal drift.
- **Rule of Weight:** Never use weights below `400`. Reserve `700` exclusively for root page titles and top-level KPI metrics. Table data and long-form records must maintain standard `400` or `500` weights to prevent visual vibration.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a strict 8pt base grid with a 4pt sub-grid for compact controls, dense tables, and input elements. 

### Grid & Viewport Architecture
- **Desktop (1280px and wider):** Fixed-fluid hybrid. The left navigation collapses between a persistent 260px expanded bar and an 80px micro-rail. The main canvas employs a 12-column grid system with 24px (`space-xl`) gutters and 32px (`space-2xl`) outer page padding.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column layout, 16px gutters, 24px margins. Navigation shifts to a collapsible overlay drawer. Complex payroll tables trigger horizontal scrolling with sticky primary identity columns (e.g., Employee Name / ID).
- **Mobile (below 768px):** 4-column layout, 12px gutters, 16px margins. Data grids transition from traditional rows to modular stacked key-value cards.

### Density Tiers
- **Standard Density:** 48px row heights for executive summaries, settings, and employee self-service.
- **Compact Data Density:** 36px row heights for mass payroll entry, batch deductions, and tax auditing tables to maximize records above the fold.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is conveyed through **tonal layering and low-contrast hairline borders**, intentionally avoiding heavy, theatrical drop shadows. Enterprise operators require low eye-strain surfaces that feel physically anchored.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (App Shell Background):** `#F8FAFC`. The foundational canvas behind cards and workspaces.
- **Level 1 (Structural Cards & Data Panels):** `#FFFFFF`, bounded by a single 1px solid `#E2E8F0` border. No shadow is applied under resting state; separation is achieved purely through color transition.
- **Level 2 (Hover States, Popovers & Contextual Menus):** `#FFFFFF` with a 1px solid `#CBD5E1` border and an ambient shadow: `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 2px 4px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Slide-over Drawers):** `#FFFFFF` framed by a 1px solid `#94A3B8` hairline border, cast over a 40% `#0F172A` dimmed backdrop. Shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The design system enforces a disciplined, conservative corner radius (Soft / Level 1). High-density enterprise applications risk wasted screen real-estate and visual disorientation when corners are overly rounded.

- **Inputs, Buttons, Badges, and Micro-controls:** 4px (`0.25rem`) border radius. This delivers an engineered, crisp appearance that feels secure and technical.
- **Cards, Panels, and Drawers:** 6px to 8px (`0.375rem` to `0.5rem`) max border radius to preserve clean horizontal and vertical lines across complex grid layouts.
- **Strict Prohibition:** Full-pill shapes are prohibited except for miniature numerical count indicators (e.g., notification counts or pending approval counts).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#0F172A`, text `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#0F172A`. On hover, background transitions to `#1E293B`. Height: 36px (compact) or 40px (default). Typography: `body-md` / weight 600.
- **Secondary / Operational:** Background `#FFFFFF`, text `#334155`, border 1px solid `#CBD5E1`. On hover, background shifts to `#F8FAFC` and border to `#94A3B8`.
- **Tertiary / Action Indigo:** Background `#2563EB`, text `#FFFFFF`. Reserved for final execution commands like "Submit Payroll" or "Disburse Funds." Hover: `#1D4ED8`.
- **Ghost:** Background transparent, text `#64748B`. Hover: `#F1F5F9`, text `#0F172A`.

### Data Tables
- **Header:** Height 36px, background `#F8FAFC`, bottom border 1px solid `#E2E8F0`. Text formatted in `label-md`, uppercase, tracking `0.04em`, color `#64748B`.
- **Rows:** Height 40px (compact) or 52px (standard). Alternating rows retain `#FFFFFF`, with hover state shifting immediately to `#F8FAFC`. Active selection row receives a 2px vertical border `#2563EB` on the left anchor and a `#EFF6FF` tint.
- **Numeric Cells:** Right-aligned, set in `tabular-numeric` using JetBrains Mono.

### Badges & Status Chips
- **Geometry:** Height 22px, border-radius 4px, padding 2px 8px. Font: `label-sm`.
- **Success (Processed / Compliant):** Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#065F46`, border 1px solid `#A7F3D0`.
- **Warning (Action Pending / Discrepancy):** Background `#FFFBEB`, text `#92400E`, border 1px solid `#FDE68A`.
- **Danger (Failed / Legal Block):** Background `#FFF1F2`, text `#9F1239`, border 1px solid `#FECDD3`.
- **Neutral (Draft / Inactive):** Background `#F1F5F9`, text `#475569`, border 1px solid `#E2E8F0`.

### Form Controls & Inputs
- **Base Input:** Height 36px, background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#CBD5E1`, text `#0F172A`. Placeholder `#94A3B8`.
- **Focus State:** 1px solid `#2563EB` with an external 2px box-shadow ring in `rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)`. No default browser rings.
- **Error State:** Border 1px solid `#E11D48`, accompanied by a 12px error note beneath the control.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 16px square/circle with 1px solid `#CBD5E1`. Selected state filled with `#0F172A` and white check icon.

### Cards & Container Panels
- **Architecture:** Solid `#FFFFFF` fill, 1px perimeter border `#E2E8F0`. Header bands include an optional 48px high title block bordered at the bottom by 1px solid `#F1F5F9`.

### Drawers & Sliding Audit Rails
- **Slide-overs:** Right-anchored side sheets used for deep employee profiles, tax breakdown summaries, and audit histories. Fixed width of 480px or 640px, anchored with a 1px solid `#E2E8F0` left separator border and Level 3 elevation.

### Login View (Enterprise Portal Entry)
- **Composition:** Split-screen layout (Dark Navy `#0B1528` showcase column + Pure White `#FFFFFF` enterprise auth container).
- **Showcase Elements:** Global precision metrics (99.98% accuracy, 42,000+ slips), automated treasury clearance with SOC2 Type II compliance badge, customer testimonial block, and regulatory trust footer (GDPR, ISO 27001, FinCEN).
- **Auth Form Controls:** Enterprise email with SSO indicator, master password with visibility toggle and 4-tier strength bar, session persistence checkbox with FIDO2 badge, primary workspace sign-in action (`#000000`), and Fast Sandbox simulation tabs connected to real backend auth.
